Not on the phone
The rules list and the heat map. One step becomes three phone screens: see what is waiting → read one request → note and decide. Editing a rule is desktop-only.
Person: What is waiting on me?
App: Twelve overdue, twenty-seven due today. The largest is a new supplier request for four hundred and twelve thousand, raised three days ago. Do you want the overdue list?
Person: Yes.
App: Reading the first three of twelve. Say “next three”, or say a requester's name.
What voice cannot do here
Recording a decision needs a note, and above the threshold it needs a second approval. Dictating that has not been agreed, so voice stops at reading. If this step ever needs twenty-six prompts, the step is too big — that is the test, and it is why the device tabs exist.
Structure only — not a visual design, not real data, nothing here is proof that anything works. Sample values are listed in Data and fields and are examples, never requirements.
